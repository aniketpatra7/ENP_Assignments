================================================================================
TASK 6 - Recommendation (derived from simulation results)
================================================================================
  * Bit rate: 500 kbit/s - slowest rate meeting all worst-case
    deadlines (with 1 error) AND leaving >= 25% cable-length margin.
  * ID allocation: reserve lowest IDs for safety-critical frames
    (Airbag 0x040, ABS 0x080); assign higher IDs to non-critical traffic.
  * Network segmentation: isolate infotainment/comfort traffic on a
    separate bus behind a rate-limiting gateway.
  * Load & verification: keep safety-bus utilisation below ~30-40% and
    validate with worst-case response-time analysis under fault models.
  * Fault tolerance: rely on CAN TEC/REC bus-off confinement, heartbeat
    timeouts, and a bus guardian. If higher throughput is needed, adopt
    CAN FD (robust arbitration rate, faster data phase).
